Solar battery management enclosure

Electronics integration IoT project 3D Printing

Component packaging & spatial planning

Designing the enclosure required precise spatial planning to house an LC filter, a CC-CV buck converter, and three USB modules within a compact footprint.

Internal standoffs and mounting features were modeled to securely fit each component, ensuring perfect alignment with the external cutouts for the voltage/current displays, solar input, battery output, and safety switch.

Thermal management & safety

Power electronics, particularly the CC-CV buck converter handling the solar input and battery output, generate continuous heat under load. Proper thermal dissipation was a primary requirement.

Passive cooling vents were strategically positioned to allow natural convective airflow around the power components, preventing thermal degradation and ensuring safe operation alongside the integrated hardware safety switch.

DFM and mechanical assembly

The enclosure was engineered strictly for FDM 3D printing. Cutouts for the USB ports and monitoring screens were optimized to print cleanly without requiring internal supports.

The assembly utilizes robust screw bosses with calibrated tolerances for brass threaded inserts, allowing the case to be repeatedly opened for maintenance without stripping the plastic threads.
