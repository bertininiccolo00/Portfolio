Engineering first, modeling second.

My models aren't just surfaces — they come from a mechanical engineering background, which means tolerances, load paths, and manufacturability get considered from the first sketch, not bolted on afterward.

That mindset comes from two places: my progression from structures to technical lead on a competitive university rocketry team, where a bad model meant a part that failed on the pad, and my ongoing work in the railway sector at Te.Si.Fer. Srl, where executive technical drawings and signaling schematics demand absolute accuracy and strict adherence to industry standards.

I bring that same rigor to my freelance CAD work. I currently collaborate with a furniture studio on organic, sculptural forms, and with an independent maker on parametric, print-ready product design—creating stackable organizers, modular shelving, and load-bearing supports built with fully editable parameters in Fusion 360 for scalable manufacturing.

I'm looking to take on fewer, better projects: longer collaborations, clearer scopes, and clients who value precision as much as I do.
