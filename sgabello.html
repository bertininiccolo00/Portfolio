Rocket motor mount

Mechanical Design Structural support 3D Printing

Airframe integration & load transfer

This thrust structure acts as the critical interface between the solid-propellant motor and the rocket airframe. The primary design objective was to safely transfer axial launch loads while minimizing overall mass.

To achieve this, I modeled a ribbed, conical geometry that effectively distributes the thrust vector from the inner motor tube to the outer fuselage wall, preventing localized buckling.

Dynamic FEM validation

To ensure structural integrity during the flight profile, the geometry was validated through Finite Element Analysis (FEA) directly within the CAD environment.

By applying the specific thrust curve of the motor as a dynamic load, I identified peak stress concentrations. This allowed for iterative optimization of the rib thicknesses and fillets, achieving the required safety factor without adding unnecessary weight.

SLS manufacturing in PA12

The final component was manufactured using Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) in PA12 Nylon. This process and material were specifically chosen for their isotropic mechanical properties and thermal stability near the motor casing.

The CAD model included calibrated tolerances to account for the SLS process, ensuring a precise, structural slip-fit assembly with both the centering rings and the external airframe.
